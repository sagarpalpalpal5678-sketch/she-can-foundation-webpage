Who We Are

About She Can
Foundation

She Can Foundation is a non-governmental organisation dedicated to dismantling the invisible walls that hold women back — poverty, inequality, lack of education, and social exclusion. Founded on the belief that the upliftment of women is the upliftment of all humanity, we work at the grassroots level to bring real, lasting change.

We are mentors, advocates, and companions in the journey of thousands of women across underserved communities. Through education, skill-building, healthcare access, and legal aid, we ensure no woman is left behind.

Our Ambition

Our Mission & Vision

We envision a world where every woman — regardless of her background — has the freedom, tools, and dignity to author her own story. Our mission drives every programme we run.

01

Educate & Enlighten

We believe education is the most powerful equaliser. Our mission includes eliminating illiteracy among women in rural and urban underserved areas through accessible and quality learning opportunities.

02

Economically Uplift

Financial independence transforms lives. We connect women with micro-financing, job placement programmes, and entrepreneurship mentoring so they can build sustainable livelihoods.

03

Eradicate Violence

Safety is a fundamental right. We run awareness campaigns, crisis helplines, and support shelters to protect women from gender-based violence and advocate for stronger legal protections.

04

Leadership Pipeline

We prepare the next generation of women leaders through mentorship, leadership academies, and by placing women at decision-making tables in government, business, and civil society.

05

Health & Dignity

Access to healthcare is not a privilege. We organise medical camps, maternal health clinics, and mental wellness sessions so every woman can live in full health and dignity.

06

Community Building

Change is collective. We foster safe community spaces where women can connect, share experiences, support one another, and amplify their collective voice in society.

Our Goals

What We're
Working Towards

Every action we take is guided by clear, measurable goals designed to create systemic change for women across communities.

🎓

Educate 50,000 Women by 2027

Enrol underprivileged women into formal and informal education programmes, closing the gender literacy gap in target districts.

🌱

Launch 500 Women-Led Enterprises

Support women entrepreneurs with seed grants, mentoring, and market access to build sustainable businesses.

🛡️

Zero Tolerance for Gender Violence

Establish rapid-response units and partner with law enforcement to ensure every survivor has immediate support and justice.

🏛️

30% Women in Local Leadership

Advocate and train women to take up roles in local governance, panchayats, and community boards across all operating regions.

💊

Universal Health Access

Ensure every woman in our programme areas has access to regular health screenings, maternal care, and mental health resources.
